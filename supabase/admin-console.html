Roadtrip Trivia Support

Look up a player, reset access, credit rounds, or record a refund. Staff sign-in uses an email account, not the Supabase dashboard login. There is no “update profiles” button under Authentication.

  1. Open the Roadtrip Trivia users page. Click Add user, then Create new user. Enter your email and a password, and turn on Auto Confirm User.
  2. Open Table Editor in the left sidebar (not Authentication). Select the profiles table. Find the row with your email and set is_admin to true. Save the row.
  3. Come back here and sign in with that same email and password.
